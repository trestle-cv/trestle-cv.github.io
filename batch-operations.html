Records

Batch operations

Create up to 100 records in one all-or-nothing SQLite transaction.

POST /api/v1/collections/issues/records/batch
Content-Type: application/json

{"records":[
  {"values":{"title":"One"}},
  {"values":{"title":"Two"}}
]}

If any record fails validation or conflicts with a physical constraint, none of the records are committed. Validation paths include the failing item index, for example records[1].values.title.

Bounded work

Empty batches and batches larger than 100 records are rejected. Request bodies are limited to 1 MiB.

Trestle is being built in the open.
