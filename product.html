Product

A compact backend without a closed ecosystem.

Trestle is designed for browser and mobile clients operating through collection rules, and trusted application backends using scoped service identities.

Planned capabilities

Collections and records

Typed schemas, relations, validation, transactions, filters and deterministic pagination.

Authentication and rules

Application users, service accounts and operation-specific access rules with explainable evaluation.

Files and realtime

Local or S3-compatible storage plus durable, replayable Server-Sent Events.

Operations and automation

Audit, backup, durable jobs, signed webhooks and AWS Lambda invocation from committed events.

Explicit boundaries

Clustering, PostgreSQL, GraphQL, hosted cloud and in-process user scripts are not first-release commitments.

Trestle is being built in the open.
