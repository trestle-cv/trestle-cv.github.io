Application authentication

Application users have their own identities, password hashes and rotating sessions. They are never administrators.

POST /api/v1/auth/register
POST /api/v1/auth/login
POST /api/v1/auth/refresh
POST /api/v1/auth/logout

Passwords require seven Unicode characters and use Argon2id. Login failures are deliberately generic. Refresh tokens are random, stored only as hashes, expire after 30 days, rotate on every use and can be revoked server-side.
