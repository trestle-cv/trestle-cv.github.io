Credentials

Personal tokens

Personal tokens are attributable administrator tooling credentials with the same create-once and scope boundaries as services.

req, _ := http.NewRequest("GET", url, nil)
req.Header.Set("Authorization", "Bearer "+token)
res, err := http.DefaultClient.Do(req)

The stored credential points to its administrator owner. Revoking it does not revoke the administrator's browser sessions or unrelated credentials.
