Queries

Relation expansion

Relation fields are queryable as stored record IDs. Embedded relation expansion is not enabled yet.

Clients should fetch related records explicitly for now. This keeps cycles, authorization and response-size behavior honest while the rule engine is still being built.

Planned boundary

A later checkpoint will add bounded expansion depth, cycle detection, per-related-record authorization and deterministic omission behavior. Trestle does not currently accept an expand parameter.
