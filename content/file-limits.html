Limits and quotas

The initial local backend bounds individual and aggregate storage.

  • Maximum object: 10 MiB.
  • Default local quota: 100 MiB.
  • Maximum stored filename metadata: 255 characters.
  • Multipart bodies are bounded before parsing.

The dashboard reports current usage. Quota is checked before and during streaming so an upload cannot exceed the remaining allowance.
