Signed webhooks

Webhook targets subscribe to event topics and deliver through the durable job engine with retry, dead-letter visibility and versioned HMAC envelopes.

Headers

Trestle-Delivery: del_...
Trestle-Timestamp: 178...
Trestle-Signature: v1=hex-hmac-sha256

Compute HMAC-SHA256 over timestamp + "." + rawBody using the copy-once signing secret, compare in constant time, reject stale timestamps and deduplicate delivery IDs.

Envelope

{"version":"1","id":"del_...","topic":"record.created",
 "collection":"issues","recordId":"rec_...","payload":{}}

A non-2xx response, timeout or connection failure retries through the ordinary job backoff policy. Delivery remains at least once.
